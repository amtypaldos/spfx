Global

Sends MIDI clock (ring) and Pocket Operator pulses (tip) from the SP-1's sync jack at the BPM above. The on/off state is remembered by the device.

Loud and Line out can hurt your ears on headphones. Line out is for a powered speaker or mixer input only. Levels are calculated, not measured. Going louder first lowers the volume so nothing jumps; raise it slowly with VOL+. Every power-up starts at Headphones, and plugging something into the jack also returns to Headphones.
